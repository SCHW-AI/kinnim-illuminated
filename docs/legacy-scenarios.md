## Case (a): One חטאת and One עולה

### Setup
- Bird 1: designated חטאת.
- Bird 2: designated עולה.
- Before the event, observers know which bird is which.

### Events
1. **Mix.** The two birds are mixed together. The truth is unchanged, but observers no longer know which bird is the חטאת and which is the עולה. Each bird becomes a "?".

### Ruling logic
A חטאת's blood goes below the חוט הסקרא and an עולה's above it, so a bird that is definitely one of the two but of unknown identity cannot be offered either way. After the mix, both birds are ספיקות. Nothing is kasher: both birds (2 of 2) must be left to die.

### Result title
Both are ספיקות - They must die

### Explanation
A חטאת needs to have its blood placed below the חוט הסקרא and an עולה needs to have its blood placed above the חוט הסקרא. Therefore, if a bird is definitely a חטאת or definitely an עולה but we don't know which, it must be left to die. In this scenario, both birds are a ספק and must be left to die.

## Case (b): Many חטאות and One עולה

### Setup
- Many birds, each a designated חטאת (the original portrayal showed 24).
- One bird: designated עולה.
- Before the event, observers know which bird is which.

### Events
1. **Mix.** The עולה joins the group of חטאות. The truth is unchanged, but observers no longer know which bird is the עולה. Every bird becomes a "?".

### Ruling logic
The majority does not help: living things are not בטל, so the single עולה is not nullified among the many חטאות. Every bird is a ספק. Nothing is kasher: all birds (the whole mixture) must be left to die. The reverse case (many עולות and one חטאת) has the same ruling.

### Result title
All are ספיקות - They must all die

### Explanation
Even though there are many חטאות and only one עולה, the birds are living beings, so the עולה does not become בטל in the חטאות. Therefore, all of the birds are now ספיקות and must be left to die. This would also be true in the opposite case of many עולות and one חטאת.

## Case (c): קן סתומה + One חטאת

### Setup
- A קן סתומה: two birds (one will be a חטאת and one an עולה, but neither is designated yet).
- One separate bird: designated חטאת.

### Events
1. **Mix.** The קן סתומה and the designated חטאת are mixed into one group of three. The truth is unchanged, but observers no longer know which bird is the designated חטאת and which two are the undesignated birds of the קן.

### Ruling logic
Exactly one bird may be offered, as a חטאת. Whichever bird is picked works: if it is the designated חטאת, it is a valid חטאת; if it is an undesignated bird of the קן, offering it as a חטאת designates it so. A second חטאת cannot be offered: if the first pick was an undesignated bird, the other undesignated bird is now the עולה. No עולה can be offered: the pick might be the designated חטאת. Result: 1 bird is kasher (as a חטאת), 2 birds must die.

### Result title
1 חטאת can be brought, 2 birds must die

### Explanation
**Why 1 חטאת can be brought:**

No matter which bird we select as a חטאת, it is valid:

- If we pick the defined חטאת → it's a valid חטאת
- If we pick either undefined bird → bringing it as a חטאת defines it as such

**Why we cannot bring a second חטאת:**

If the first bird was undefined bird A (now defined as חטאת), then undefined bird B is now an עולה. Bringing it as a חטאת would mean bringing an עולה as a חטאת - invalid.

**Why we cannot bring any עולה:**

The bird we select might be the defined חטאת, so we would be bringing a חטאת as an עולה - invalid.
